ABOUT ROGER

Business experience. Technical education. Practical solutions.

I'm Roger Fifield, a freelance software and web developer with a Bachelor of Science in Business Administration from the University of Phoenix and an associate degree in Graphic Design and Advertising from Dutchess Community College.

During my business degree, I pursued substantial computer science and programming coursework alongside business subjects including management, finance, economics, teamwork, presentations, critical thinking, and mathematics. My technical studies covered programming foundations, advanced programming, networking, databases, and web development. I've studied and worked with technologies including HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Java, Python, Ruby, C++, C#, React, and Tailwind CSS. I continue refreshing and developing those skills through further study and hands-on projects.

My background also includes owning and operating a business. I bring together business understanding, design knowledge, and technical skills to help clients develop useful websites and digital tools. I describe portfolio demonstrations and work in progress clearly, and I agree on project scope before taking on client work.
